# Hypothesis

OPM's public workforce-data site may present government-wide workforce and administrative-leave indicators while its current EHRI release records unresolved missing components and known historical reporting errors. The September 18 comparison narrowed the question: OPM labels the administrative-leave KPI as EHRI Payroll, but the public downloads, table builder, and API reviewed today expose only Status/Dynamics-derived datasets and do not expose the Payroll denominator needed for an agency- or month-level comparison.

This remains a bounded hypothesis, not a claim that the KPI is inaccurate. The current documents establish a public-access limitation and OPM's disclosures; they do not establish the size or direction of any effect on the 145,130 figure.
