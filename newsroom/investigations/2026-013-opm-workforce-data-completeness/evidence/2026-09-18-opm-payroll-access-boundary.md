## Excerpts

- OPM's Compensation, Performance & Leave page identifies the administrative-leave KPI's source as “OPM EHRI Payroll,” reports 145,130 employees for a biweekly pay period starting in late June 2025, and labels the page “Data as of July 2026.”
- OPM's Data Sources page says Payroll records include time reported by employees, including leave, and are collected biweekly. It lists Status, Dynamics, Payroll, and Training as EHRI feeds.
- OPM's public Data Downloads page says it provides record-level data only from EHRI Status and Dynamics, then lists July 2026 Employment, Accessions, and Separations files. Its opening description does not offer Payroll records.
- OPM's Table Builder says it creates tables only from EHRI Status and Dynamics datasets; its dataset selector lists Employment, Accessions, and Separations, with no Payroll option.
- OPM's API Access page says programmatic access is open for Employment, Accessions, and Separations and lists only those three datasets and download endpoints. The July 2026 Employment list endpoint returned `employment_202607_1`, published September 2, 2026; no Payroll endpoint is documented.

## Why it matters

This is a documented boundary, not proof that the 145,130 figure is wrong. The public pages identify Payroll as the metric's source, while the public record-level downloads, table builder, and API expose only Status/Dynamics-derived datasets and the Employment, Accessions, and Separations interfaces. The investigation cannot compute an agency- or month-level denominator for the leave metric from the public endpoints reviewed today.
