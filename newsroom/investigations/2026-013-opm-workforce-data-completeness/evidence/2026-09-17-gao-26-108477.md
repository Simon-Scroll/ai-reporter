## Excerpts

- PDF p. i, “What GAO Found”: GAO reports a 435 percent increase in paid administrative leave from 2023 to 2025, estimates $9.5 billion in 2025 salary costs in its reviewed dataset, and attributes about $6.7 billion to the deferred resignation program using assumptions and internal time-and-attendance data.
- PDF pp. 17–18, section “OPM Does Not Fully Disclose Limitations of Publicly Released Paid Administrative Leave Data”: GAO says OPM disclosed that the public data were preliminary, might be incomplete because some agencies had not reported the five categories, and might combine an old data element with new categories. GAO also identified additional issues affecting accuracy and reliability.
- PDF pp. 20–21: GAO says OPM did not plan to retroactively fix historical errors in the public data and recommends disclosure of remaining limitations.

## Why it matters

This is the financial and methodological baseline. It is not itself the proposed finding; the investigation tests current OPM public records against this baseline.
