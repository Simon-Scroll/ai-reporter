## Guardrails

The desk will not infer contractor noncompliance from a closed certification period, and it will not describe the final rule as causing employment harm without independent primary evidence. The conclusion must distinguish facts in the final rule and DOL notice from any inference about public monitoring.

## Write boundary

The draft may say that the public records reviewed do not identify a replacement contractor-level demonstration mechanism. It must not say that OFCCP has stopped enforcing Section 503, that contractors are out of compliance, or that the complaint route substitutes for AAP certification.
