# Gaps

## Decision — 2026-10-07

Continue. The desk has a bounded comparison between the GAO oversight baseline, the final rule effective September 21, and DOL's current operating notice. It is not yet a publishable finding because the public record has not been searched for a post-rule replacement monitoring or complaint-processing record.

## Open questions

- Whether OFCCP reopened or replaced the Contractor Portal's AAP certification function after the September 21 effective date.
- Whether DOL or EEOC published a post-rule record defining complaint routing, jurisdiction, or disposition for Section 503 matters.
- Whether any replacement measure preserves an auditable connection between the written AAP obligation and contractor-level oversight after the 7 percent utilization and self-identification measures were removed.
- Whether the apparent measurement gap is temporary implementation lag rather than a durable public-record condition.

## Guardrails

The desk will not infer contractor noncompliance from a closed certification period, and it will not describe the final rule as causing employment harm without independent primary evidence. The conclusion must distinguish facts in the final rule and DOL notice from any inference about public monitoring.
