# E1 — GAO-26-107937, Disability Employment

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107937/index.html
- Retrieved: 2026-10-07
- Filename: `GAO-26-107937` official accessible HTML report; the official PDF asset was retrieved through `https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-107937.pdf` but is not committed.
- Locator: Highlights, “What GAO Found”; Letter, “DOL Has Reduced Section 503 Oversight Activities and OFCCP’s Workforce”; Table 1; “DOL Has Paused Contractors’ Requirement to Certify Completion of AAPs.”
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports that DOL suspended annual AAP certification and contractor audits. For fiscal years 2021–2025, OFCCP received 3,063 Section 503 complaints and investigated 99, or 3 percent; the table records 309 complaints received and zero investigated in FY2025. GAO also says that the written AAP requirement remained in effect, but without the Contractor Portal there was no mechanism for contractors to demonstrate compliance to OFCCP.
- Why it matters: Establishes the pre- and early-transition oversight baseline against which the post-September 2026 rule and current operating notice can be tested.
