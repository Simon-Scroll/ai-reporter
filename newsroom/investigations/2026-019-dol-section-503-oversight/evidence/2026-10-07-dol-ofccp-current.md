# E3 — DOL current OFCCP operating notice

- URL: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ofccp
- Retrieved: 2026-10-07
- Locator: “Executive Order 14173” page, paragraphs beginning “In response to E.O. 14173” and “Because OFCCP's compliance review format significantly entangled.”
- Captured excerpt: DOL's page says Order 08-2025 lifted the abeyance for Section 503 and VEVRAA activity, but says OFCCP administratively closed all pending compliance reviews and took no further action on the November 2024 scheduling list. It also says the Section 503 and VEVRAA AAP certification period remains closed while OFCCP revises its processes and systems, although the statutory and regulatory obligations remain in effect.
- Why it matters: Supplies a current post-rule agency statement that can be compared with the final rule's continuing AAP requirement and the monitoring baseline reported by GAO.
