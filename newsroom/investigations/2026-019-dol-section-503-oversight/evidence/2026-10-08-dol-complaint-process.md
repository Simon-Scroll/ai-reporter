# E4 — DOL OFCCP complaint process

- URL: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ofccp/contact/file-complaint
- Retrieved: 2026-10-08
- Locator: “Pre-Complaint Inquiry,” “Complaint,” and “IMPORTANT DEADLINES” sections.
- Captured excerpt: The page says pre-complaint inquiries and employment-discrimination complaints may be submitted online, by emailing a PDF form to OFCCPComplaintForms@dol.gov, or by mail. It says complaints must be filed with OFCCP within 300 calendar days of the alleged action and that a filer with 60 days or fewer remaining should bypass the pre-complaint process.
- Why it matters: Supplies the current public complaint-intake route. It describes individual employment-discrimination complaints, not a replacement mechanism for contractor-level annual AAP certification or demonstration.
