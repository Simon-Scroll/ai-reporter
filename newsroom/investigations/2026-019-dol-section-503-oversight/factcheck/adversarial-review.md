# Adversarial review — 2026-10-08

## Claims that survive

- C1's complaint and investigation counts are tied to GAO's “What GAO Found,” Letter, and Table 1 locators. The article preserves GAO's distinction between the written-AAP obligation and the missing Contractor Portal demonstration mechanism.
- C2 is a comparison claim grounded in the final rule's effective-date, executive-summary, and Section 503 implementing-regulations sections. It does not describe the final rule as causing harm.
- C3 uses the current OFCCP operating notice for the closed certification period, closed reviews, and complaint-processing statements.
- C4 uses the current complaint-process page's named sections for the intake channels and 300-day deadline.
- C5 is properly bounded as a statement about the public pages reviewed. It does not assert that no internal or future mechanism exists.
- C6 labels the conclusion as an inference and expressly rejects contractor noncompliance and a claim that OFCCP stopped processing complaints.

## Claims that die

- No claim is killed outright. The article must not call the complaint page a replacement for AAP certification, and it must not claim the complaint page itself is dated after September 21; the draft does neither.

## Remaining gaps

- The complaint-process page does not expose a page-update date. The draft therefore calls it current by retrieval date and does not treat it as proof of when DOL changed the page.
- The reviewed pages do not establish whether OFCCP has a nonpublic or not-yet-linked contractor-level AAP demonstration process.
- The rule's removed self-identification and data-collection measures must not be broadened into a claim that all disability-related records or all Section 503 enforcement ended.

## Recommendation

**Draft/hold for cadence.** The comparison is original enough to support a labeled draft: a current complaint route is documented separately from a continuing written-AAP obligation whose certification period the agency says remains closed. Do not publish before the six-day interval after the October 6 publication, and rerun the skeptic and validation gates if a new official implementation record appears.
