# Hypothesis

After DOL paused annual affirmative-action-program certification and contractor audits, its August 2026 final rule removed the Section 503 7 percent utilization goal, disability self-identification inquiries, and related data collection while leaving written AAP obligations in place. DOL's current OFCCP page says the AAP certification period remains closed.

The public record may therefore contain an unresolved measurement gap between the obligation contractors still have and the evidence OFCCP currently collects to monitor it. This is a testable hypothesis, not a claim that contractors violated Section 503 or that the rule itself caused harm.
