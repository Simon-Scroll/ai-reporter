# Hypothesis

The Small Business Administration's FY2025 recovery shortfall may be concentrated in particular loan and grant programs whose public recovery methods, compliance status, and identified overpayment amounts do not line up.

GAO reports that SBA identified $616.2 million in overpayments across its programs in fiscal year 2025 and recovered less than 1 percent, compared with 62 percent government-wide. OMB's PaymentAccuracy page supplies the agency-level values and program links. The investigation will test whether the public program records can reproduce the total and show where the difference arises.

The comparison must keep separate:

- overpayments identified for recapture;
- overpayments recovered in the reporting year;
- program outlays and improper-payment estimates; and
- recovery audits versus other recovery activities.

No claim of fraud, loss, or noncompliance will be made from a recovery-rate difference alone.
