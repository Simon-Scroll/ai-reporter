# Gaps

- GAO's agency-wide comparison is not by itself a new finding; the investigation needs a program-level comparison or a documented reporting/control difference.
- PaymentAccuracy says recovered amounts are reported in the year recovered, not the year identified. The timing rule must be applied before comparing program pages or reconstructing a rate.
- The agency page reports a single $616.18 million identified and $3.81 million recovered total, but the program pages may expose different fields, scopes, and denominators.
- The public page says SBA does not conduct payment-recapture audits for certain programs and uses other recovery methods, but the program-level amounts and method attribution are not yet joined.
- SBA's noncompliance listing and planned corrective actions are not proof that any identified overpayment was unrecovered because of a control failure.
- The six FY2025 program pages retrieved on November 12 report PIIA improper-payment estimates and categories, not the agency page's recovery-activities amounts. For example, 7(a) approvals reports $1,632.51 million in technically improper payments, while 7(a) purchases reports $17.13 million in overpayments; those figures cannot be added to or substituted for the $616.18 million recovery-activities total without a common population and method.
- The program pages do not expose program-level recovered amounts for the reporting year. Their shared FY2025 sampling period is 04/2024–03/2025, but that does not resolve the agency page's rule that recoveries are reported in the year collected.

## Decision — 2026-11-12

Parked after a same-day retrieval of six official SBA/OMB program records. The records add a documented scope boundary but do not provide a same-object program-level recovery comparison. Reopen only for a public recovery-activities breakdown or reconciliation; do not repeat these unchanged program pages.
