## Hypothesis

The Small Business Administration's FY2025 recovery shortfall may be concentrated in particular loan and grant programs whose public recovery methods, compliance status, and identified overpayment amounts do not line up. A reproducible program-level comparison could show where the agency's $616.18 million identified-overpayment total becomes only $3.81 million recovered, without inferring fraud or improper conduct from the gap alone.

## What would be new

Join GAO's FY2025 recovery benchmark to OMB's program-level PaymentAccuracy records and SBA's stated recovery-audit decisions. The finding, if supported, would be the size and location of a same-year recovery discrepancy, not GAO's own statement that SBA recovered less than 1 percent.

## Human stake

Recovery of federal overpayments affects whether public funds identified as recoverable return to the Treasury or the originating program.

## Why it might be wrong

PaymentAccuracy reports recoveries in the year collected rather than the year identified, and program pages may use different scopes or timing than GAO's comparison. The apparent gap may therefore be a reporting-period or definition difference rather than an operational shortfall.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could test one program's recovery-audit decision against its current overpayment and recovery records.
