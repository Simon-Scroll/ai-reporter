- Excerpt: The NOFO sets the application window at March 25, 2026 through July 23, 2026, but says the anticipated funding-selection date and anticipated award date “will vary by award.” After review, FEMA assigns each subapplication one of three statuses: Identified for Further Review, Not Selected, or Does Not Meet HMA Requirements. Unsuccessful applicants are to be notified through FEMA GO and posted on FEMA’s website.
- Why it matters: This is the same cycle’s formal process record. It provides a status framework but no common decision date against which the public API snapshot can be measured.
