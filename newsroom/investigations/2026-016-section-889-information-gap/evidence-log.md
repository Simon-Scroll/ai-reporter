# Evidence log

## E1 — GAO-26-108630

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108630.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23
- Locator: GAO-26-108630, pp. 18–19, 24–25, and appendix I pp. 32–33.
- Excerpt: GAO reports that GSA and DoD officials rely on company representations in SAM; almost 90 percent of companies with FY2025 federal contract activity represented that they did not use covered equipment, while representation data was unavailable for about 3,800 companies. GAO says the proposed FAR changes could remove the annual-representation requirement from SAM and that GSA and DoD officials did not know how the change would affect current information for purchase-card users.
- Boundary: GAO's report establishes the existing workflow and identified implementation concern; it does not document the final post-reform replacement.

## E2 — Federal Register document 2026-12559

- URL: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/23/2026-12559/federal-acquisition-regulation-revolutionary-federal-acquisition-regulation-overhaul-parts-1-2-4-33-39-40-and-53
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23
- Locator: 91 Fed. Reg. 37550, pp. 37554–37560; proposed FAR 52.240-2 at printed pp. 37624–37626.
- Excerpt: The proposal says only entity-level representations would remain in SAM, removes FAR 52.204-8 Annual Representations and Certifications, and makes Section 889 representations by submission of an offer in proposed FAR 52.240-2(c). It also retains a 72-hour disclosure process for an offeror that cannot represent compliance.
- Boundary: This is a proposed rule, not a final operating record; it does not by itself show how civilian agencies will search or compare current offer-level representations.

## E3 — Current DoD DFARS representation clauses

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.204-7016-covered-defense-telecommunications-equipment-or-services%E2%80%94representation. and https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.204-7017-prohibition-acquisition-covered-defense-telecommunications-equipment-or-services%E2%80%94representation.
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23
- Locator: DFARS 252.204-7016(c), current page change 5/7/2026; DFARS 252.204-7017(d), May 2021 provision.
- Excerpt: DFARS 252.204-7016 requires the offeror to represent whether it provides covered defense telecommunications equipment or services. DFARS 252.204-7017 directs the offeror to complete an additional representation if its annual representations and certifications in SAM say it does provide such equipment or services.
- Boundary: DoD's mission-specific clauses show a current annual-SAM dependency; they do not prove that civilian agencies lack a replacement process.

## E4 — RFO FAR Part 4

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-4
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: §§ 4.203-1(a)–(e), 4.203-2(a), and 4.208(b)–(c).
- Excerpt: SAM remains the primary method for entity-level representations and certifications. The model text excepts purchases under the micro-purchase threshold when a governmentwide commercial purchase card is used for purchase and payment; when SAM registration is not required, the contracting officer collects certain identifying information and entity-level representations and certifications when an offer or quotation is received.
- Boundary: The text does not establish that every purchase-card transaction includes the Section 889 solicitation provision or creates a public searchable offer-level record.

## E5 — RFO FAR Part 40

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-40
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: §§ 40.202(d), 40.204-2(a)–(b), and 40.205(a)–(c).
- Excerpt: The model text moves the Section 889 prohibition to FAR 40.202(d), records covered categories in SAM, and requires provision 52.240-90 in all solicitations and clause 52.240-91 in solicitations and contracts.
- Boundary: It prescribes a solicitation and contract workflow but does not identify a public database for offer-level representations.

## E6 — GSA RGO-2026-01

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/page_file_uploads/RGO-2026-01.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: RGO-2026-01, §§ 1–5 and Attachment A.
- Excerpt: GSA directs its acquisition workforce to use applicable Revolutionary GSAR Overhaul deviation text instead of the codified GSAR. The class deviation applies to all GSA contracting activities and procurements and remains in effect until rescinded or incorporated into the GSAR.
- Boundary: This is a GSA acquisition-workforce instruction, not a governmentwide searchable offer repository.

## E7 — HHS FAR Class Deviation 2025-09 Amendment 2

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/page_file_uploads/HHS_RFO_Deviation_Part-4-8-12-38-40-51.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: FAR Class Deviation 2025-09 Amendment 2, §§ 1, 4, 7.A–E, and 9.
- Excerpt: HHS directs all divisions to use the RFO model text for applicable actions and tells contracting officers that SAM updates may lag policy updates; they are to rely on representations from offers based on the solicitation, while entities cannot remove the displaced representations from SAM.
- Boundary: HHS demonstrates one civilian implementation path; it does not prove that every civilian agency or purchase-card action creates a public searchable record.
