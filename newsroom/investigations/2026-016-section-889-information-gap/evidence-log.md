# Evidence log

## E1 — GAO-26-108630

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108630.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23
- Locator: GAO-26-108630, pp. 18–19, 24–25, and appendix I pp. 32–33.
- Excerpt: GAO reports that GSA and DoD officials rely on company representations in SAM; almost 90 percent of companies with FY2025 federal contract activity represented that they did not use covered equipment, while representation data was unavailable for about 3,800 companies. GAO says the proposed FAR changes could remove the annual-representation requirement from SAM and that GSA and DoD officials did not know how the change would affect current information for purchase-card users.
- Boundary: GAO's report establishes the existing workflow and identified implementation concern; it does not document the final post-reform replacement.

## E2 — Federal Register document 2026-12559

- URL: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/23/2026-12559/federal-acquisition-regulation-revolutionary-federal-acquisition-regulation-overhaul-parts-1-2-4-33-39-40-and-53
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23
- Locator: 91 Fed. Reg. 37550, pp. 37554–37560; proposed FAR 52.240-2 at printed pp. 37624–37626.
- Excerpt: The proposal says only entity-level representations would remain in SAM, removes FAR 52.204-8 Annual Representations and Certifications, and makes Section 889 representations by submission of an offer in proposed FAR 52.240-2(c). It also retains a 72-hour disclosure process for an offeror that cannot represent compliance.
- Boundary: This is a proposed rule, not a final operating record; it does not by itself show how civilian agencies will search or compare current offer-level representations.

## E3 — Current DoD DFARS representation clauses

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.204-7016-covered-defense-telecommunications-equipment-or-services%E2%80%94representation. and https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.204-7017-prohibition-acquisition-covered-defense-telecommunications-equipment-or-services%E2%80%94representation.
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23
- Locator: DFARS 252.204-7016(c), current page change 5/7/2026; DFARS 252.204-7017(d), May 2021 provision.
- Excerpt: DFARS 252.204-7016 requires the offeror to represent whether it provides covered defense telecommunications equipment or services. DFARS 252.204-7017 directs the offeror to complete an additional representation if its annual representations and certifications in SAM say it does provide such equipment or services.
- Boundary: DoD's mission-specific clauses show a current annual-SAM dependency; they do not prove that civilian agencies lack a replacement process.
