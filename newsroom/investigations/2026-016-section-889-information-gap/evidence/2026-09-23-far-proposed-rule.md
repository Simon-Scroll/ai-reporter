- Locator: 91 Fed. Reg. 37550, pp. 37554–37560; proposed FAR 52.240-2 at printed pp. 37624–37626
- Excerpt: The proposal says only entity-level representations would remain in SAM, removes FAR 52.204-8 Annual Representations and Certifications, and makes Section 889 representations by submission of an offer in proposed FAR 52.240-2(c). It retains a 72-hour disclosure process for an offeror that cannot represent compliance.
- Boundary: This is a proposed rule, not a final operating record; it does not show how civilian agencies will search or compare current offer-level representations.
