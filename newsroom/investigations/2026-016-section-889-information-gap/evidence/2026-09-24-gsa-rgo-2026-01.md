# Evidence — GSA RGO-2026-01

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/page_file_uploads/RGO-2026-01.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: RGO-2026-01, §§ 1–5 and Attachment A.
- Why it matters: This is GSA's current class-deviation instruction for implementing the Revolutionary GSAR Overhaul and establishes the scope of the GSA-specific bridge.
- Excerpt: The memorandum says it approves GSAR class deviations for GSA contracting activities, directs the GSA acquisition workforce to follow applicable RGO part-deviation text instead of the codified GSAR, and states that the deviation applies to all GSA contracting activities and procurements. It also says the RGO text is posted under the FAR Overhaul link and that each deviation remains effective until rescinded or incorporated into the GSAR.
- Boundary: RGO-2026-01 is a GSA acquisition-workforce instruction. It does not itself create a governmentwide searchable repository of civilian offer-level Section 889 representations.
