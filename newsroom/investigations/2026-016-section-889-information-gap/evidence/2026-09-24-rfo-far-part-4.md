# Evidence — RFO FAR Part 4

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-4
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: §§ 4.203-1(a)–(e), 4.203-2(a), and 4.208(b)–(c).
- Why it matters: This is the current FAR-overhaul model text governing the relationship between SAM, entity-level representations, offers, and governmentwide purchase-card micro-purchases.
- Excerpt: Section 4.203-1(a) says SAM is the primary method for collecting entity-identifying information, entity-level representations and certifications, and payment/reporting information. Section 4.203-1(b)(1) excepts purchases under the micro-purchase threshold when a governmentwide commercial purchase card is used for purchase and payment. Section 4.203-1(e) says that when SAM registration is not required, the contracting officer must collect certain identifying information and entity-level representations and certifications when an offer or quotation is received. Section 4.208 prescribes the relevant SAM or offer provisions according to whether registration is required.
- Boundary: The text does not say that every purchase-card transaction carries the Section 889 solicitation provision or creates a public, searchable offer-level record.
