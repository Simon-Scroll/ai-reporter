# Evidence — RFO FAR Part 40

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-40
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: §§ 40.202(d), 40.204-2(a)–(b), and 40.205(a)–(c).
- Why it matters: This is the current governmentwide model text that relocates Section 889 and prescribes the replacement representation and contract clause.
- Excerpt: Section 40.202(d) identifies the Section 889 telecommunications and video-surveillance prohibition. Section 40.204-2(a) says covered equipment or services under paragraphs (1) and (2) of the definition will be recorded in SAM, while paragraph (b) says the paragraph (4) category is recorded by DoD in SAM. Section 40.205(a) requires provision 52.240-90, “Security Prohibitions and Exclusions Representations and Certifications,” in all solicitations; § 40.205(b) requires clause 52.240-91 in all solicitations and contracts, subject to the alternate in paragraph (c).
- Boundary: The model text prescribes a solicitation and contract workflow; it does not identify a public database where offer-level representations can be searched, including for purchases exempt from SAM registration.
