# Evidence — GSA Section 889 information collection

- URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-19/pdf/2026-21255.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-11-11
- Locator: Federal Register, vol. 91, no. 200, October 19, 2026, pp. 66150–66151; sections B and C.
- Why it matters: This is a new official GSA record checked for a current agency-specific workflow or a searchable offer-level record.
- Excerpt: GSA says it is extending OMB Control No. 3090-0322 without changes until the RGO rulemaking is completed. For GSA lease acquisitions it estimates 3,100 responses and 4,650 burden hours for FAR 52.204-24, and 62 responses and 93 burden hours for FAR 52.204-25. It describes the legacy Section 889 representation and prohibition clauses but does not identify a public searchable repository for the resulting records.
- Boundary: The notice concerns GSA lease acquisitions and information-collection burden; its response counts are not a governmentwide purchase-card denominator and do not resolve the published article's public-searchability question.
