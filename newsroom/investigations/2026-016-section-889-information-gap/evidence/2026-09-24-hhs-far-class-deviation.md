# Evidence — HHS FAR Class Deviation 2025-09 Amendment 2

- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/page_file_uploads/HHS_RFO_Deviation_Part-4-8-12-38-40-51.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24
- Locator: FAR Class Deviation 2025-09 Amendment 2, §§ 1, 4, 7.A–E, and 9.
- Why it matters: HHS publishes an agency-level implementation record showing how a civilian agency operationalizes the RFO text while the SAM system catches up.
- Excerpt: The deviation applies immediately to all HHS divisions for applicable solicitations, contracts, and orders. Section 7 directs HHS staff to follow the RFO model text and, when using RFO provisions or clauses, to include this notice: “System updates may lag policy updates. The System for Award Management (SAM) may continue to require entities to complete representations based on provisions that are not included in this solicitation. Contracting officers will rely on representations from offers based on provisions in the solicitation. Entities are not required to, nor are they able to, update their entity registration to remove these representations in SAM.”
- Boundary: HHS's instruction is evidence of one civilian agency's implementation. It does not prove that every civilian agency or purchase-card transaction produces a public searchable record.
