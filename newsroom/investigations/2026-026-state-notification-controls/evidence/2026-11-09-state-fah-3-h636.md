# State 4 FAH-3 H-636

- URL: http://fam.state.gov/fam/04FAH03/04FAH030630.html
- Retrieved: 2026-11-09
- Locator: 4 FAH-3 H-636(c), “Letters of Agreement (LOA)”
- Claim IDs: C5, C7

Short excerpt:

> The February 18, 2026 revision says records for a foreign-assistance Letter of Agreement should include the signed agreement, annex or approval document, project proposal, action memo, “Relevant Congressional Notification,” and allotment cables.

Why it matters:

This is current Department guidance for the internal award-file record set. It establishes the fields a public or internal crosswalk would need to join authority, activity, and financial execution, but it does not itself expose a particular award file.
