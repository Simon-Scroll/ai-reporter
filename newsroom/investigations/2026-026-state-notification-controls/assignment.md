## Finding test

Can a reader trace a congressional-notification authority from the specific program activity described in the notification to a later official allocation, obligation, and expenditure record, and did State's 2026 guidance or notification practice repair the link?

Keep separate:

- the legal authority cited in a congressional notification;
- the activity and program narrative in that notification;
- fund-level allocation, obligation, and expenditure data; and
- the presence or absence of a public crosswalk between those records.

Do not characterize the underlying foreign-assistance activity as unlawful or improper from a documentation gap alone.
