# Hypothesis

State's congressional-notification process may identify legal authorities and broad program narratives without preserving a public, reproducible link from the authority to the specific activity and later financial execution.

The initial same-object join is Congressional Notification 24-210. GAO reproduces the notification's citation to the FY2023 democracy-assistance notwithstanding authority, its $1.8 million Mozambique plan, and its activity narrative. State's later FY2025 Prevention and Stabilization Fund report lists the same CN number with current allocation, obligation, and expenditure amounts, but labels the program `NA`.

This is a bounded records hypothesis. It does not assert that the authority was misused, that the activity was unlawful, or that a fund-level accounting table must reproduce the notification's narrative.
