# Gaps

## Decision — 2026-11-14

**Continue, then write.** The first six-year draft repeated GAO's national endpoint trend and overstated the tables as money received. The adversarial review killed that framing. A revised calculation sums the Kentucky, Pennsylvania, and West Virginia rows across all six OSMRE tables and finds $40,415,895 of summed annual recommendation-to-distribution-designation differences, or 57.7% of the six-table difference. The article labels this as a desk calculation, states that final totals cite congressional appropriations, and does not infer a shortfall, debt, misuse, or operational harm.

## Remaining gaps

- The six-table national denominator is not a fixed panel: Oklahoma changes eligibility after FY2021, and FY2022 excludes a Tennessee program-development grant and possible Oklahoma Indian-lands grants.
- The comparison does not establish awards, obligations, state backfill, or a causal connection to inspection, reclamation, or environmental outcomes.
- A later state-level workload and outcome record could test whether the distribution differences affected a defined program result.

## Write boundary

The published piece may report the reproducible state-level aggregation and its limits. It must call the annual differences distribution designations, not money received or lost, and must not characterize permit counts as a measure of need or claim an operational failure.
