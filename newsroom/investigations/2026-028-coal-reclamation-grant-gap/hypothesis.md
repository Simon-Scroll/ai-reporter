# Hypothesis

OSMRE's Title V coal-regulatory grant distributions have covered a shrinking share of the agency's own state and Tribal program recommendations, leaving a growing dollar gap while regulatory authorities still oversee thousands of permits.

The test keeps separate:

- state and Tribal three-month estimates or regional recommendations;
- OSMRE's final distribution, which the agency says does not itself award or provide funds;
- appropriations and other funding sources; and
- the operational population of permits, including reclamation and bond-forfeiture statuses.

No claim of waste, misuse, environmental harm, or unlawful conduct will be made from a funding gap alone. The question is whether the primary records show a measured, persistent mismatch and what the records do or do not establish about its consequences.
