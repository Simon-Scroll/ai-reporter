# OSMRE coal-regulatory grant gap

## Assignment

Test whether OSMRE's Title V coal-regulatory grant distributions have covered a shrinking share of its own state and Tribal program recommendations while regulatory authorities continue to oversee thousands of permits. The first comparison will calculate the recommendation-to-distribution ratio and dollar gap from OSMRE's FY2021–FY2026 tables, then join the FY2026 result to GAO's permit, reclamation, forfeiture, staffing, and grant-capacity records.

## What would be new

The underlying OSMRE tables permit a six-year calculation of the gap's size and trend. The article, if supported, would quantify the mismatch and its operational context rather than repeat GAO's statement that the gap grew.

## Human stake

Funding supports state and Tribal permitting, inspection, and enforcement for coal operations and reclamation. The FY2026 table shows a $16.7 million difference between OSMRE's regional recommendations and final distribution.

## Why it might be wrong

OSMRE says a distribution designates funds but does not itself award them, and available funds may be distributed equitably when they do not cover total needs. A persistent gap may therefore be an appropriations limit, not evidence of misuse or an operational failure. GAO's permit data are from spring 2025 while grant tables cover different fiscal years.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could test the gap against a specific state's inspection, reclamation, bond-forfeiture, or permit-workload records.
