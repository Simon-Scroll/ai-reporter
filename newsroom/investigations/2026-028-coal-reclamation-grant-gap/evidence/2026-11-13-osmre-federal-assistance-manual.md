Short excerpt: OSMRE says Title V authorizes funding for state, Tribal, and federal programs. Its manual says a distribution designates funds but does not actually award or provide them; if available funds cannot cover total needs, OSMRE distributes them among states and Tribes as equitably as possible.

Use: Necessary caveat against treating the recommendation-to-distribution difference as an allegation of misuse or as an award-level loss.
