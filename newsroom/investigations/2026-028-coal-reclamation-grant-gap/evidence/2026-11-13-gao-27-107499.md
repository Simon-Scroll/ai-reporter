Short excerpt: GAO reports 5,247 coal-related permits as of spring 2025, including 1,838 in reclamation and 908 in bond forfeiture. It says federal regulatory grants fell from $68.6 million in FY2021 to $63.7 million in FY2026, while the gap between OSMRE's recommended and actual distribution grew from $4.2 million in FY2021 to $16.7 million in FY2026.
