The six-year sums are $254,748,017 recommended and $214,332,122 final, a summed annual difference of $40,415,895. All six table totals sum to $457,163,311 recommended and $387,090,000 final, a $70,073,311 difference. The selected states' share is 57.65%, rounded to 57.7%.

The comparison is not an accumulated debt or money lost. OSMRE's footnotes cite congressional appropriations for final totals; its FY2022 footnotes also exclude a Tennessee Program Development grant and possible Oklahoma Indian-lands grants. Oklahoma appears with a FY2021 recommendation and final distribution but has no recommendation or final distribution in later tables.
