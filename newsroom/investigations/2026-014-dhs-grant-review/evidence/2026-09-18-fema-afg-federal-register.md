## Excerpts

- p. 57629, summary and dates: FEMA describes the FY2025 Assistance to Firefighters Grant and Fire Prevention and Safety application processes; applications ran May 19–June 22, 2026.
- p. 57629, supplementary information: FEMA says it received 8,426 AFG applications and expected approximately 1,800 awards, and 758 FP&S applications and approximately 100 awards.
- pp. 57629–57630, congressional appropriations: $291.6 million was made available for FY2025 AFG awards and $32.4 million for FY2025 FP&S awards; funds were available for obligation and award through September 30, 2026.
- pp. 57630–57631, application evaluation and review: FEMA describes financial-integrity review, competitive ranking, technical review, and final award ranking.
- p. 57636, adopted recommendations: FEMA states that period-of-performance extensions and amendment requests were updated for FY2025.

## Boundary

This notice is a current FEMA grant-process primary, but it concerns AFG and FP&S rather than the USCIS Citizenship and Integration population. It does not reconcile the USCIS award-level discrepancy in GAO-26-109097 and is not used as proof that no FEMA termination memorandum exists.
