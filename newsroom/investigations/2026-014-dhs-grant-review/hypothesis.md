# Hypothesis

## Working hypothesis

DHS's 2025 grant review left a reproducible gap between component-level termination accounting and the public award records used to audit it, while FEMA's 2026 rolling-termination process may repeat the same weakness without a documented lessons-learned control.

## Test

Compare the GAO termination table with the underlying USAspending records and current FEMA process documents for one selected program. Treat a missing or zero API result as a data-availability observation until award identifiers, date windows, and component naming are reconciled.

## Boundaries

- GAO's own conclusions are not a finding by themselves.
- Do not infer unlawful conduct or intent from a grant pause, termination, or data mismatch.
- Do not claim a public record is absent until the relevant official interface and alternate document path have been tested.
