# Timeline

- 2018 — The Navy established SIOP; NAVFAC's current program page describes the original Report to Congress estimate as $21 billion over 20 years.
- 2026-09-25 — GAO published GAO-26-107830, reporting that SIOP would surpass $200 billion and take more than 50 additional years based on Navy plans, and that consolidated standardized cost and risk reporting was not required.
- 2026-10-01 — The desk opened this investigation after comparing the GAO report with the Navy's FY2027 budget presentation and NAVFAC's current SIOP FAQ.
- 2026-10-02 — The detailed FY2027 budget listed six named SIOP construction lines totaling $1.256 billion by desk calculation. USAspending supplied project-level records for P1062 Dry Dock 3 Modernization and P454 Multi-Mission Dry Dock development, but their contract measures did not provide a common full-program baseline.
- 2026-10-03 — USAspending directly identified the Pearl Harbor execution record as FY23 MCON Project P-209 Dry Dock 3 Replacement and reported $3.071 billion in total obligations and $3.298 billion in base and all options. The House FY2027 appropriations report directed a future SIOP waterfront-facilities cost and timeline report; no enacted authorization or consolidated program baseline was found.
