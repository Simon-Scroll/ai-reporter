# Assignment

## Question

Can the public record connect the Navy's FY2027 SIOP funding and project list to a current, consolidated program cost and performance baseline for the four public shipyards?

## Scope

This is a document comparison about program traceability, not an allegation of waste or misconduct. The initial record compares GAO-26-107830, the Navy's FY2027 budget presentation, and NAVFAC's current SIOP program page. A publishable result requires a specific, reproducible gap between the annual funding/project record and the cost, schedule, risk, or performance information needed to evaluate the same program.

## Selection

- Public interest: 5/5
- Originality: 3/5 after excerpts from GAO, the Navy FY2027 budget, and NAVFAC's SIOP page
- Document-nativeness: 5/5
- Room to grow: 5/5
- Legal risk: 1/5
- Net: 17/25

The possible finding is not that SIOP is delayed or wasteful. It is whether a reader can reconcile the Navy's current annual request and project count with a current full-program baseline when the agency's own program page says a full cost estimate is not yet available.

## Primary documents to fetch first

1. GAO, [Naval Shipyards: Complete Information Needed for Decision-Making on Multibillion-Dollar, 50-Year Infrastructure Program](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107830).
2. Department of the Navy, [President's Budget (PB) 2027](https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/27pres/DON_Press_Brief.pdf).
3. NAVFAC PEO IE/PMO 555, [Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program](https://www.navfac.navy.mil/PEO-Infrastructure-and-Expeditionary/PMO-555-SIOP/).

## Human stake

The four public shipyards maintain the Navy's nuclear-powered fleet, while SIOP is a multidecade infrastructure commitment involving hundreds of billions of dollars in projected work.

## Why it might be wrong

The Navy may have a fuller internal baseline or standardized project records that are not exposed on the public overview pages. The apparent gap may also reflect the difference between an annual budget request and a long-range capital program, not an inconsistency.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could test a named SIOP project against its authorization, obligation, schedule, and execution records.
