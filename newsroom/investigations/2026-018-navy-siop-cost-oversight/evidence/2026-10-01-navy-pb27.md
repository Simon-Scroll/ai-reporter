# Evidence E2 — Navy FY2027 SIOP request

- URL: https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/27pres/DON_Press_Brief.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01
- Document: Department of the Navy, *President’s Budget (PB) 2027*
- Locator: “$8.7B Maritime Industrial Base & SIOP by Program Type ($B)” slide and “Installations, Facilities, and Military Construction” slide.
- Excerpt: The presentation lists “$1.8B for Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program (SIOP) to expand shipyard capacity and optimize shipyards.” Its installations and facilities section says the FY2027 military-construction request invests in 64 projects, including six SIOP projects, and says the Navy continues to support SIOP.
- Why it matters: This is the current annual funding and project-count record to compare with the long-range program baseline and reporting requirements.
- Boundary: The budget presentation is a request, not an enacted appropriation or an execution record; the $1.8 billion figure is not treated as the program’s total cost.
