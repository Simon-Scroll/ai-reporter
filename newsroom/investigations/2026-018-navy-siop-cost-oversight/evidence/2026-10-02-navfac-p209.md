# Evidence E9 — NAVFAC Pearl Harbor project record

- URL: https://pacific.navfac.navy.mil/Facilities-Engineering-Commands/OICC-Pearl-Harbor-Naval-Shipyard/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-02
- Record: Official OICC Pearl Harbor Naval Shipyard page
- Locator: “Mission,” “Dry Dock 5 Information,” and “Dry Dock 5 Construction Progress Update: August 2026.”
- Excerpt: The page identifies OICC Pearl Harbor as delivering SIOP facilities and says its work begins with Dry Dock 3 Replacement (P-209). In the project information section it describes a $2,839,880,250 firm-fixed-price task order, N62742-23-F-4007, for a concrete dry dock, says work is expected to be completed by September 2027, and identifies the work as part of SIOP.
- Why it matters: This is a current project-level cost and schedule record, but the page’s project labels require reconciliation with the FY2027 budget’s “Dry Dock 3 Replacement Increment” and its $507 million request line. The public record does not present a single cross-year baseline explaining the relationship between the request, the task order, and the page’s separate “Dry Dock 5” heading.
- Boundary: The page is not treated as proof of a discrepancy or delay. Its labels are recorded as published and require a same-object check before any article claim.
