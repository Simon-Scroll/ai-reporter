# Evidence E3 — NAVFAC current-cost disclosure

- URL: https://www.navfac.navy.mil/PEO-Infrastructure-and-Expeditionary/PMO-555-SIOP/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01
- Document: NAVFAC PEO Infrastructure and Expeditionary / PMO 555, *Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program*
- Locator: FAQ, “Q2: The initial 2018 Report to Congress projected SIOP as a $21-billion, 20-year program. What is today’s estimate?”
- Excerpt: NAVFAC says the early estimate will be higher than the 2018 projection, but that “additional items impact the program’s ability provide a full cost estimate at this time,” including changing economic conditions, infrastructure degradation, facility and shop reconfiguration, and sequencing and interdependency of investments.
- Why it matters: The Navy’s own current public program page provides the historical baseline and expressly says a full current cost estimate is not yet available.
- Boundary: The FAQ does not state whether a separate internal estimate exists, and it does not supply project-level obligations, schedules, or performance data.
