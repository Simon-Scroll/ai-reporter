# Evidence E5 — FY2027 SIOP project table

- URL: https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/27pres/Highlights_Book.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-02
- Document: Department of the Navy, *FY 2027 Budget Highlights Book*
- Locator: Section IV, “Military Construction,” SIOP discussion and Figure 4.2, “Quality of Life, Warfighter Readiness and New Platform & Technologies Funding Summary.”
- Excerpt: The SIOP discussion names five projects and the Figure 4.2 table lists six SIOP construction lines: Dry Dock 3 Modernization, Portsmouth VA ($189 million); Dry Dock 3 Replacement, Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam HI ($507 million); Multi-Mission Dry Dock, Kitsap WA ($245 million); Electrical Distribution System Upgrades, Portsmouth VA ($125 million); Power Reliability & Water Resilience Upgrades, Portsmouth Naval Shipyard ME ($139 million); and Multi-Mission Drydock 1 Extension, Portsmouth Naval Shipyard ME ($51 million).
- Desk calculation: Those six listed amounts total $1.256 billion. The document does not label that sum as the SIOP total; it is a calculation of the six named construction lines.
- Why it matters: The detailed budget exposes a project-level set of six amounts, but it does not by itself show how those lines map to the separate $1.8 billion SIOP headline or to a current full-program cost and risk baseline.
- Boundary: This is a FY2027 budget request. It is not an enacted appropriation, an obligation record, or a full lifecycle estimate.
