# Evidence E1 — GAO SIOP oversight findings

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107830
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01
- Document: GAO-26-107830, *Naval Shipyards: Complete Information Needed for Decision-Making on Multibillion-Dollar, 50-Year Infrastructure Program*
- Locator: Product page, “What GAO Found,” paragraphs beginning “The Navy’s Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program (SIOP) will surpass $200 billion” and “Further, while the Navy provides Congress with reports and briefings.”
- Excerpt: “The Navy’s Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program (SIOP) will surpass $200 billion and take over 50 more years to execute, based on GAO’s analysis of Navy plans.” GAO also says the Navy does not provide Congress with consolidated, standardized information on full program costs and risks because no requirement exists to do so.
- Why it matters: This establishes the external baseline and the reporting gap to test against current Navy budget and NAVFAC records.
- Boundary: The product page summarizes GAO’s analysis; it does not by itself establish whether later Navy project records provide the missing standardized baseline.
