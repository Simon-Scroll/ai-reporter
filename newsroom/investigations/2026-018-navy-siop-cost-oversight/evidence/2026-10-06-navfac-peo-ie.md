# Evidence E13 — Current NAVFAC program-management disclosure

- URL: https://www.navfac.navy.mil/PEO-Infrastructure-and-Expeditionary/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-06
- Document: NAVFAC PEO Infrastructure and Expeditionary, “What is a PEO?”
- Locator: PEO IE overview, “What is a PEO?”
- Excerpt: The page says PEO IE and PMO 555 oversee “$6.3 billion in ongoing construction, design, planning, and environmental work” and says SIOP integrates design, construction, budgeting, contracting, and staffing to manage cost, schedule, and risks.
- Why it matters: The current public program page supplies a large work-in-progress figure and describes a management framework, but it does not publish a table reconciling that figure to the FY2027 $1.8 billion headline, six construction lines, O&M equipment activity, and contract-level measures.
- Boundary: The page does not establish that the $6.3 billion figure is a total program cost, an enacted appropriation, or an execution total for any single project.
