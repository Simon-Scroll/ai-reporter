# Evidence E6 — FY2027 SIOP funding lines outside the six-project table

- URL: https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/27pres/OMN_Book.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-02
- Document: Department of the Navy, *FY 2027 President’s Budget Submission, Operation and Maintenance, Navy*
- Locator: “Base Support,” “Sustainment, Restoration and Modernization,” “IV. Performance Criteria and Evaluation Summary”; and “Other Procurement, Navy,” “Industrial Plant Equipment Program.”
- Excerpt: The performance table reports FY2027 SIOP funding of $92.124 million under Sustainment, Restoration and Modernization. The Other Procurement section says the SIOP FY2027 OPN budget funds recapitalization of industrial plant equipment, including cranes, test stands, cutters, generators, mills, and security systems, but does not state a single SIOP dollar total for that equipment list in the passage.
- Why it matters: The annual SIOP headline spans more than the six construction lines, while the public budget documents place related facilities and equipment funding in separate sections with different accounting labels. The captured records do not provide a single cross-appropriation reconciliation table.
- Boundary: This record describes requested funding, not enacted or executed amounts.
