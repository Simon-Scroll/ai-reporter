# Evidence E11 — House FY2027 military-construction report

- URL: https://www.congress.gov/committee-report/119th-congress/house-report/622/1
- Retrieved: 2026-10-03
- Document: H. Rept. 119-622, *Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies Appropriations Bill, 2027*
- Locator: “Military Construction, Navy and Marine Corps” and “Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program (SIOP) Waterfront Production Facilities”
- Excerpt: The committee recommends $5,508,034,000 for Navy and Marine Corps military construction in FY2027 and itemizes $73,000,000 for the listed projects. In the SIOP passage, it directs NAVFAC and NAVSEA to provide a joint report within 180 days of enactment covering a long-term cost-benefit analysis for waterfront production facilities at each public shipyard, timelines and estimated costs for planned SIOP waterfront facilities, and actions to accelerate construction.
- Why it matters: The congressional record adds a future reporting requirement for cost, timeline, and estimated-cost information rather than supplying the consolidated project baseline sought in this investigation.
- Boundary: This is a House committee recommendation, not an enacted appropriation. The report’s $5.508 billion account recommendation is not treated as a SIOP total.
