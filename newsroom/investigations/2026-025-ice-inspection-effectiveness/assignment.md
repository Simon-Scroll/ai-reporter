## Finding test

Can a reader connect ICE's stated inspection goals and current standards to a public measure of whether changed inspection frequency and self-inspection methods identify deficiencies or improve conditions as the detained population and facility count increase?

The work must keep separate:

- facility counts and average daily population;
- inspection frequency and inspection method;
- deficiencies, corrective actions, contract compliance, and facility conditions; and
- ICE's reported goals versus evidence used to evaluate their effects.

The investigation must not infer mistreatment, unlawful detention, or noncompliance from an oversight gap alone.
