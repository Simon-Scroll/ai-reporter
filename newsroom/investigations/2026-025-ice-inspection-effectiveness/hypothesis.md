# Hypothesis

ICE changed detention-facility inspection frequency and methods while the detained population and facility count increased, but the public record may not connect its stated inspection goals and standards to measurable evidence about whether the changes identify deficiencies or improve facility conditions.

GAO-25-107580 found that DHS entities lacked clear performance goals and measures for assessing inspection-program effectiveness. GAO-27-109299 reports that ICE later changed inspection frequency and increased reliance on facility self-inspections, while not using deficiency and contract-performance evidence to determine the effects of those changes. ICE's 2026 National Detention Standards and a July 2026 ODO inspection report provide the current policy and facility-level records against which to test that comparison.

This is a bounded records hypothesis, not a finding that detention conditions are unlawful, unsafe, or worse because inspection methods changed.
