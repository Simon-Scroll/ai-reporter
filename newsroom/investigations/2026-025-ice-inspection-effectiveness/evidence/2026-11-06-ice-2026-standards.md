# Evidence E4 — ICE 2026 National Detention Standards

- URL: https://www.ice.gov/detain/detention-management/2026
- Retrieved: 2026-11-06
- Locator: Opening paragraph
- Excerpt: ICE says it revised the National Detention Standards in 2026 to streamline them, reduce the burden on detention operators, and align them more closely with the United States Marshals Service's Federal Performance Based Detention Standards and the Non-Dedicated Intergovernmental Service Agreement Standards issued March 26, 2025.
- Why it matters: Identifies the current standards revision and its stated purpose; it does not by itself establish inspection effectiveness.
