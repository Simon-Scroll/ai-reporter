# Evidence E5 — Monroe County Detention Dorm ODO inspection

- URL: https://www.ice.gov/doclib/foia/odo-compliance-inspections/MonroeCoDetDorm_MonroeMI_Jul14-16_2026.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-11-06
- Locator: “Inspection Process,” pp. 2–3
- Excerpt: The July 14–16, 2026 report says ODO conducts annual on-site inspections for dedicated facilities, biennial on-site inspections for non-dedicated facilities with an average daily population of 50 or more, and an initial or biennial ODO-assisted self-inspection process for certain lower-population non-dedicated facilities. It says ODO staff observe conditions, interview detainees, and spot-check reported findings during the self-inspection process.
- Why it matters: Provides a dated operational example of the inspection methods described at the agency level; it cannot be generalized to all facilities.
