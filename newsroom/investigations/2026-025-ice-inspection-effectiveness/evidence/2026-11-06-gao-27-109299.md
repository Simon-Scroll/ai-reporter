# Evidence E2 — GAO-27-109299

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-109299
- Retrieved: 2026-11-06
- Locator: “Fast Facts”; “What GAO Found”
- Excerpt: GAO reports that from April 2025 to April 2026 ICE added more than 40 facilities holding noncitizens for over 72 hours and the average daily detained population grew by more than 50 percent to 61,817. It also reports that inspection frequency changed, reliance on facility self-inspections increased, and ICE had not used deficiency data and contract-performance information to determine the effects of the changes.
- Why it matters: Supplies the later agency-wide inspection-change and evidence-use record for comparison with GAO-25-107580.
