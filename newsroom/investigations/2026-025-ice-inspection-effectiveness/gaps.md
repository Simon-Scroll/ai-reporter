- GAO-25-107580 records ICE's January 2026 report of strategic performance goals for the Office of Detention Oversight and planned measures for the ICE Health Service Corps, but the public page says documentation or follow-up remained unresolved as of May 2026.

- The number of facilities and average daily population cannot be used as a denominator for inspection coverage until the reports' facility populations, time periods, and inspection categories are aligned.
- A facility-level deficiency or corrective action cannot be generalized to the national program.

## Decision required

Continue while an official ICE or DHS goal, performance measure, inspection dataset, recommendation-response record, or comparable facility series can connect inspection method to deficiency detection, corrective action, or contract-performance outcomes. Park if a serious same-day official retrieval finds only the two GAO summaries and descriptive standards without a joinable outcome measure. Do not publish an accusation of deficient care from the current record.
