# Evidence — Fort Leonard Wood IGSA snapshot

- URL: https://www.army.mil/article/283409/igsa_partnerships_help_garrison_take_care_of_warfighters_while_saving_millions
- Retrieved: 2026-11-10
- SHA-256: e70679c2d8978b1629a1bb57ae07c8d734f51c73238f3a84645780b033af6f76
- Locator: Army article dated February 27, 2025; paragraphs beginning “Fort Leonard Wood currently has six IGSAs” and “The fire extinguisher agreement is the second IGSA signed this year.”
- Excerpt: The Army says Fort Leonard Wood had six IGSAs and that three recent agreements were projected to save more than $320,000 per year and more than $3.2 million over their lives. It says the fire-extinguisher IGSA would save more than $90,000 per year and that a trash-and-recycling IGSA was estimated to save more than $200,000 annually and more than $2 million over ten years.
- Boundary: This is an official public snapshot of projections and signed-agreement announcements, not a signed approval letter, cost-benefit analysis, realized-savings calculation, statutory certification, or installation-level recapture record. The figures are not directly comparable to the June 2026 aggregate of annual savings and cost avoidances without agreement-level definitions and periods.
