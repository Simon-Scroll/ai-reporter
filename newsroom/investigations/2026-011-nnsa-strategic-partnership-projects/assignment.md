# Assignment: NNSA Strategic Partnership Projects and cost transparency

## Hypothesis

NNSA's Strategic Partnership Projects may have expanded faster than the public records needed to show that sponsor-funded work was approved, fully cost-recovered, and separated from DOE's appropriated mission work.

## What would be new

The finding would connect a defined SPP project's approval, sponsor funding, contract accounting, actual costs, and closeout, or show a specific gap between those required records.

## Human stake

SPP work uses national laboratories, plants, facilities, and specialized staff while public funds and sponsor payments must remain traceable and protected from cross-subsidy.

## Why it might be wrong

GAO's review may identify only management opportunities, while site-level contract and financial records may show that approval, advance funding, cost recovery, and reporting controls operate as designed.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could compare SPP execution across NNSA sites or trace one sponsor-funded project from approval through contract accounting and closeout.
