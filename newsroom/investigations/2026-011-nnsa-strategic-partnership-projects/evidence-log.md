## E2 — DOE Order 481.1E, Strategic Partnership Projects

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/documents/archivedoe-o-481-1estrategic-partnership-projects-formerly-known-work-others-non-doe
- Retrieved: 2026-09-13
- Locator: paragraphs 4.c–e, 5.k(11)–(13), 6.b(2), and 6.f; PDF/document pages 4, 12–14.
- Excerpt: Before accepting an SPP agreement, DOE requires written determinations and certification that the work is mission-consistent, does not directly compete with the domestic private sector, and satisfies applicable authority. Field elements must establish performance measures, maintain a summary of each active agreement with sponsor and estimated-cost fields, and retain policy-compliance documentation. NNSA's budget office is responsible for maintaining financial information and preparing reports as necessary.
- Boundary: The order states required controls; it does not show whether a particular project complied.

## E3 — DOE Financial Management Handbook Chapter 13.1

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2022-10/doe-financial-management-handbook-chapter-13.1-reimbursable-work-and-interagency-agreements.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-13
- Locator: pages 3–7, sections I.A–I.C and II.A–II.C; pages 15–18, sections IV.B.1–IV.B.7.
- Excerpt: DOE requires reimbursable agreements to be managed within agreement-specific funding limits, prohibits DOE appropriations from financing non-DOE work unless authorized, requires advance funding for most nonfederal work, and calls for final-cost determination and a final invoice or return of unused funds at closeout.
- Boundary: The handbook is a department-wide financial baseline and does not establish the execution record for an individual NNSA SPP.

## E4 — NNSA contract Section J, Appendix O

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/sec-j-appendix-o-program-management-and-cost-reports
- Retrieved: 2026-09-13
- Locator: RFP No. 89233222RNA000004, Section J, Appendix O, pages 1–2.
- Excerpt: The contract reporting attachment requires monthly cost, schedule, and technical reports, including actual-versus-budgeted costs, schedule performance, and estimated cost at completion, plus annual spend plans, schedule plans, and work-scope specifications.
