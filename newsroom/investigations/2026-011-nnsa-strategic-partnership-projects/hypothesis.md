# Hypothesis

NNSA's Strategic Partnership Projects may have expanded faster than the public records needed to show that sponsor-funded work was approved, fully cost-recovered, and separated from DOE's appropriated mission work.

The first primary comparison is not a finding. GAO's September 2026 review describes Strategic Partnership Projects performed by NNSA's contractor-managed sites for other federal agencies and nonfederal entities. DOE Order 481.1E requires pre-acceptance determinations and written certification, field-level performance measures, active-agreement summaries, and records documenting policy compliance. DOE's financial handbook separately requires advance authority, agreement-specific controls, and customer funding for reimbursable work.

The investigation will test whether a defined project or site has public records linking approval, sponsor funding, contract accounting, actual costs, and closeout. It will not infer cross-subsidy, improper work, or noncompliance from GAO's program-level description.
