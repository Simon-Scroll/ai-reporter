## What was tested and did not explain the obligation gap

The Army file's base-and-exercised-options value is $854,490,135.68. Subtracting the $253,178,228.81 obligation leaves $601,311,906.87. That remainder is $2,867,035.83 more than the ICE obligation of $598,444,871.04. The ceilings differ by $2,867,036.51. Those near-matches are not the same number, and neither Army figure equals the ICE obligation.

The Army account-obligation list is empty and `total_account_obligation` is 0. That does not affirmatively prove Treasury removed $253,178,228.81. The ICE account obligation matches the ICE contract obligation. The files therefore do not show two live Treasury balances of $253.2 million and $598.4 million.
