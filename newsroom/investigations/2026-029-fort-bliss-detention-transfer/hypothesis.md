# Hypothesis

ICE order 70CDCR26FR0000001 describes itself as the administrative transfer of Army order W9124J25FA075 for the Fort Bliss / Camp East Montana detention center. Those two public contract files may not carry the same total obligated amount, and neither file may show the modification that would make one figure reproduce the other.

This is a test of the funded-obligation field on the transferred order. It is not a test of GAO's separate statement that ICE later awarded a different operating contract for about $452 million.
