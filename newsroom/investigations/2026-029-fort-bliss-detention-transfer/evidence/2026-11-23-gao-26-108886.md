Excerpt: "In July 2025, the Army awarded and administered a task order on behalf of ICE" for the Fort Bliss facility. "The Army contract modification on September 23, 2025, transferred contract administration responsibilities to ICE at the start of fiscal year 2026." "The Army awarded a detention facility service contract valued at up to $1.3 billion." "The Army exercised the first option year in September 2025 before transferring contract administration responsibilities to ICE." ICE later terminated that contract for convenience and, on March 12, 2026, awarded a noncompetitive contract to a different vendor.

Use: The prior GAO report identifies the September 23 modification as the transfer and gives a ceiling of up to $1.3 billion. It does not state $253,178,228.81 or $598,444,871.04. It does not rescue a subtraction of those two obligations.
