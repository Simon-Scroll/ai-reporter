# Gaps

- GAO's public product record gives aggregate component totals, but the full report's selected grant-level records and any underlying DHS termination file still need to be joined to award identifiers.
- USAspending exposes award-level obligation, funding, outlay, and performance-period fields, but the initial query does not identify whether the selected award was terminated, paused, reinstated, or included in GAO's 362-grant population.
- FEMA's BRIC page documents a 2024–25 application cycle and says earlier January 2025 subapplications will not be reviewed; it does not by itself establish a termination or lessons-learned control.
- “Deobligation,” “current award amount,” “outlay,” and “savings” are not interchangeable measures.
