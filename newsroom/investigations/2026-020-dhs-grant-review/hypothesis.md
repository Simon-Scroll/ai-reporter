# Hypothesis

DHS's 2025 grant review left a reproducible gap between component-level termination accounting and the public award records used to audit it, while FEMA's 2026 rolling-termination process may repeat the same weakness without a documented lessons-learned control.

GAO-26-109097 reports that four DHS components terminated 362 grants and deobligated about $1.002 billion between January 20 and September 30, 2025, with FEMA accounting for 215 grants and $999.433 million. The public question is whether those aggregate actions can be joined to stable award identifiers and current transaction fields, and whether FEMA's later process records show a documented control change.

This is an opening hypothesis, not a finding. The USAspending and FEMA records captured on October 13 establish testable comparison paths but do not yet prove a mismatch.
