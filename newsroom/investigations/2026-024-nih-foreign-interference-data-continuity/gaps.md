# Gaps

- NIH's 2023, 2025, and 2026 briefs use different cutoff dates and contacted-case periods, so the aggregate changes are not a contradiction by themselves.
- The outcome categories overlap: serious violations, no violation found, and open reviews do not sum cleanly to the contacted-case total. They must not be treated as mutually exclusive.
- GAO's 697-allegation and 264-violation frame is broader than NIH's 263/273 contacted-case frame. The reports do not publish a common row-level identifier.
- The public NIH page links the current brief and provides aggregate tables, while the decision matrix and May 2026 update explain screening and outreach. The reviewed public materials still do not disclose a case-level change log or historical crosswalk.
- NIH's public data page says updates are semi-annual. A later brief, case-level record, or downloadable annual series could reconcile the snapshots and narrow or kill the finding.

## Decision required

The same-object test now passes for a bounded comparison: the three reports use the same top-level contacted-institution population while reporting different as-of dates, and the public process materials do not supply the record needed to reconcile the transitions. Draft and label the missing crosswalk as the finding; do not turn aggregate disclosure violations into allegations of foreign interference or unlawful conduct.
