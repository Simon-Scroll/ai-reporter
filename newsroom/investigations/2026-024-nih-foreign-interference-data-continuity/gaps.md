# Gaps

- NIH's 2025 and 2026 briefs use different cutoff dates and contacted-case periods, so the aggregate changes are not a contradiction by themselves.
- The outcome categories overlap: serious violations, no violation found, and open reviews do not sum cleanly to the contacted-case total. They must not be treated as mutually exclusive.
- GAO's 697-allegation and 264-violation frame is broader than NIH's 263/273 contacted-case frame. The reports do not publish a common row-level identifier.
- The public NIH page links the current brief and provides aggregate tables, but the reviewed page and briefs do not disclose a case-level change log or a historical crosswalk.
- NIH's public data page says updates are semi-annual. A later brief, methodology note, or downloadable annual series could reconcile the snapshots and narrow or kill the finding.

## Decision required

Continue only if a new official record can explain the population and category transitions or produce a reproducible annual series. If no such record is public after a serious same-day search, the bounded finding may be the missing crosswalk itself; do not turn aggregate disclosure violations into allegations of foreign interference or unlawful conduct.
