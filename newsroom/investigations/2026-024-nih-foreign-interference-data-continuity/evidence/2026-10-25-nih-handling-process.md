# Evidence — NIH allegation-handling process

- URL: https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/foreign-interference/handling-allegations
- Retrieved: 2026-10-25
- Locator: “Standard Procedure,” steps 1–4
- Short excerpt: NIH says potential issues can come from institutional self-disclosure, NIH staff, anonymous allegations, or law enforcement. OER reviews whether NIH-funded grants are involved and whether sufficient and verifiable information exists to proceed; when research is involved, NIH contacts the institution and reviews the response for disclosure, overlap, financial-conflict, and foreign-component issues.

Use: Explains why NIH's contacted-case count is a screened subset of allegations and why it cannot be added to GAO's broader allegation count.
