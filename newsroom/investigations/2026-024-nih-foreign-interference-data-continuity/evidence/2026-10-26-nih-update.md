# NIH May 2026 foreign-interference update

- URL: https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2026/05/an-update-on-how-nih-protects-nih-funded-research-from-undue-foreign-interference
- Retrieved: 2026-10-26
- Locator: “Foreign Interference Assessment Tool,” “Revised Disclosure Forms,” and “In Summary,” May 12, 2026
- Claim IDs: C9, C11

Short excerpt: NIH describes the 2024 decision matrix as its decision tree for when it will contact an applicant or recipient and whether mitigation may be required, recommended, or unnecessary. The update also says that current and pending support, foreign components, and affiliations are used to assess scientific, budgetary, and commitment overlap.
