# NIH November 2023 foreign-interference case brief

- URL: https://grants.nih.gov/sites/default/files/Foreign-Interference-11-26-23-report.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-26
- Locator: NIH/OER brief dated 2023-11-28, pp. 1 and 3
- Claim IDs: C7, C8

Short excerpt: The report says it covers reviews as of November 16, 2023; NIH had received allegations involving 639 scientists and staff had contacted institutions in 255 cases. Its outcome table uses the contacted-institution population, reports 214 cases with any serious violation and 16 with no violation, and the report says repayments had been received or agreed in 32 cases totaling $24,076,727.
