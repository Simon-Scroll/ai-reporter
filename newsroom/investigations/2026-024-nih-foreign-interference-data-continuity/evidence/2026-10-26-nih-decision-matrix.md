Short excerpt: NIH says the matrix is based on the Grants Policy Statement, NSPM-33 implementation guidance, and 2 C.F.R. 200.206. It describes when NIH will contact institutions for more information and states that, for most allegations, NIH closes compliance reviews internally without outreach to institutions.
