# Evidence — GAO-27-107848

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-27-107848.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-25
- Locator: pp. 27–30 and p. 77
- Short excerpt: GAO reports that from 2017 through 2025 NIH received 697 allegations of potential foreign interference, identified disclosure violations in 264 cases, and took one or more actions in 223 of those cases. The report distinguishes serious violations from undisclosed foreign affiliation alone and says its data were sufficiently reliable for reporting the volume and sources of allegations and corresponding actions.

Use: Supplies an independent primary review with a broader allegation denominator and different outcome framing. It is not added to the NIH contacted-case totals and is not treated as a reconciliation.
