# Hypothesis

NIH's public foreign-interference summaries changed their contacted-case and outcome totals between July 2025 and June 2026, but the published snapshots do not provide a row-level crosswalk showing whether the changes are newly contacted cases, resolved reviews, or reclassification. GAO's separate 2017–2025 allegation and violation counts use a broader denominator and different outcome language.

This is a records-continuity question, not a finding that NIH failed to detect foreign interference or that any researcher or institution acted unlawfully. The next test is whether NIH publishes a method, annual series, or case-level public record that reconciles the snapshots.
