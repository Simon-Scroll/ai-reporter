## Finding test

Can a reader reconcile NIH's public foreign-interference case snapshots from November 2023, July 2025, and June 2026 with GAO's 2026 review of NIH's allegation data? The three NIH snapshots show 255, 263, and 273 contacted cases, but the public summaries do not provide a row-level crosswalk explaining which cases were added, resolved, or reclassified.

The article must distinguish three populations: allegations received, institutions contacted, and violations found. It must not treat a disclosure violation as proof of foreign interference, unlawful conduct, or a security breach.

## Work rule

Keep allegation counts, contacted-case counts, serious-violation counts, no-violation counts, open reviews, and repayment totals as separate measures. Do not add overlapping outcome categories. Do not identify a person, institution, foreign country, or grant as culpable from aggregate tables. A later case-level record or methodology note that reconciles the snapshots would narrow or kill this hypothesis.
