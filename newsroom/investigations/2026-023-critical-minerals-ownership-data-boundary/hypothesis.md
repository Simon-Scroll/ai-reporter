# Hypothesis

BLM's public mining-record system may expose claimant, operator, mine-plan, mineral, and case identities without exposing the ownership and citizenship fields needed to connect statutory claimant eligibility to foreign-parent ownership for critical-mineral operations on federal land.

The first two primaries establish adjacent but not identical populations:

- GAO-27-108196 began with 236 business-entity claimants in BLM data for approved hardrock operations, then selected a nongeneralizable sample of 15 after matching against OpenCorporates and other sources. Seven of those 15 did not have evidence of U.S. incorporation in the records GAO reviewed.
- GAO-27-108094 reviewed 43 U.S.-incorporated companies that were claimants or operators associated with 59 identified critical-mineral mines on BLM-managed federal lands. GAO determined ownership for 32 and found 14 subsidiaries of foreign parents.

This is not yet a finding. The counts cannot be added, subtracted, or treated as overlapping until a public BLM record supplies a stable case, claimant, operator, mine, or serial-number join. The investigation will test whether BLM's own public access paths can perform that join and what fields they expose.
