# Evidence — BLM MLRS page

- URL: https://www.blm.gov/services/land-records/mlrs
- Retrieved: 2026-10-20
- Relevant claims: C2, C3

## Excerpts

- **“Mineral & Land Records System (MLRS)”:** BLM describes MLRS as an online platform for mineral and land records transactions, tracking, mapping, and other case functions, including mining claims and solid minerals.
- **“How can I conduct research on new areas of interest in MLRS?”** BLM says the research map lets users search public data and search by name or legal land description.
- **“Will BLM track serial numbers on my current case filings?”** BLM says existing serial numbers remain searchable and MLRS generates standardized serial numbers for existing and future cases.
- **“Where do I access MLRS reports?”** BLM directs users to https://reports.blm.gov/reports/mlrs.

## Why it matters

The page identifies public case and serial-number search capabilities that could support an identity join. It does not say that MLRS records include citizenship or foreign-parent fields; GAO-27-108196 and GAO-27-108094 say those fields are generally not collected by BLM.
