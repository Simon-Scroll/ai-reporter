## Excerpts

- **p. 2, “What GAO Found”:** GAO reviewed ownership information for 43 claimants and operators associated with critical-mineral mines on federal lands, determined ownership for 32, and found 14 subsidiaries of foreign entities.
- **pp. 11-12, “How much foreign investment occurs…”:** GAO says it reviewed 43 U.S.-incorporated companies that were claimants or operators for 59 identified critical-mineral mines on BLM-managed federal lands. It used company websites, state business records, S&P Capital IQ, and SEC databases.
- **pp. 11-12:** The 14 foreign-parent cases were U.S.-registered companies listed by BLM in MLRS as the mining claimant or operator. GAO also describes examples involving Chinese financial interests in foreign parent companies.
- **p. 12:** BLM officials said BLM does not record parent ownership or changes in parent ownership because federal mining law does not require disclosure.

## Why it matters

This report supplies a critical-mineral mine/operator ownership frame and confirms that the public BLM record labels the U.S.-registered claimant or operator, but it does not provide a crosswalk to the 236-claimant universe or the 15-claimant eligibility sample in GAO-27-108196.
