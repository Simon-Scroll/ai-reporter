# Evidence — BLM NRS and MLRS retrieval test

- NRS URL: https://reports.blm.gov/report/MLRS/130/CR-Solid-Minerals-Surface-Mining-Operations/
- MLRS portal URL: https://mlrs.blm.gov/s/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-20
- Relevant claims: C3

## Retrieval result

- The NRS page loaded the official BLM reporting shell titled “CR Solid Minerals - Surface Mining Operations: Surface Mining Operations by State,” but the page displayed “Your session has expired” and did not expose report rows without an authenticated session.
- The MLRS public portal returned HTTP 503 on the same-day request.

## Why it matters

These results record a same-day access barrier only. They do not establish that BLM lacks the records or that the two GAO populations cannot be joined. A later run should try a distinct BLM geospatial or report channel after the portal is available.
