# Evidence — BLM access to notices and plans

- URL: https://www.blm.gov/programs/energy-and-minerals/mining-and-minerals/locatable-minerals/access-mining-notices-and-plans-operations
- Retrieved: 2026-10-20
- Relevant claims: C2, C3

## Excerpts

- **“Access to Mining Notices and Plans of Operations”:** “The public can see what solid locatable mineral exploration notices and plans of operation have been submitted through two major BLM web applications: Mineral and Land Records System (MLRS) and ... National-Apps Reporting System (NRS).”
- **“National-Apps Reporting System (NRS) Reports”:** BLM says the national reports are tabular data from the MLRS system and links reports by state, case type, and geospatial data layers.
- **“How to use MLRS directly…”:** BLM says the MLRS Community public portal lets users perform searches and look up specific cases, while the Research Map can be used to research case information spatially.

## Why it matters

This is the official public-access description for the records that might supply the join. It establishes a retrieval path, not yet the existence of a claimant-to-operator-to-ownership crosswalk.
