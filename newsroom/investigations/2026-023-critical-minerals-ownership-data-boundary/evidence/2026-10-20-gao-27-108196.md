## Excerpts

- **p. 2, “What GAO Found”:** “GAO analyzed a nongeneralizable sample of 15 mining claimants that appeared to be business entities and could not find evidence that seven of the claimants were incorporated in the U.S.”
- **p. 4, “Scope and Methodology”:** “We obtained data from BLM on approved hardrock mining operations” and analyzed business-entity claimants for incorporation status.
- **p. 5, “Scope and Methodology”:** GAO says it started with 236 business-entity claimants, matched 162 to U.S.-incorporated entities in OpenCorporates, and selected all seven that matched foreign incorporation records plus eight unmatched claimants for the 15-claimant sample.
- **pp. 17-18, “BLM Generally Does Not Collect Information…”:** BLM generally does not collect citizenship information, document whether claimants meet the requirements, or verify citizenship.

## Why it matters

This report supplies a broad hardrock-operation claimant frame and a focused eligibility sample. It does not supply the foreign-parent ownership population or a public crosswalk to GAO-27-108094's critical-mineral mine operators.
