# Hypothesis

NNSA's FY2026 budget requested funding for 2,003 federal FTEs while GAO reports 1,755 federal employees at the end of the second quarter of FY2026; the difference may show that the agency's staffing plan and its reported workforce had diverged during a period of expanding nuclear-modernization work.

This is a records comparison, not a finding that NNSA failed a mission, breached a staffing requirement, or created a safety risk. The key question is whether the two documents describe the same federal workforce and whether the request was a planned level, a funded ceiling, or an expected end-state.
