# E5 — NNSA Savannah River Site contract workforce-planning requirements

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/sec-j-app-s
- Retrieved: 2026-10-18
- Locator: “2.1 Staffing Plan” and “6.1 Workforce Plan” in Section J, Appendix S.
- Excerpt: The contractor must provide NNSA an evaluation of the current workforce, a plan for the right workforce size and skills mix, and an estimate of employees to whom it expects to make offers. The annual workforce plan must analyze current and future mission requirements across the Future Years Nuclear Security Program and address critical skills, hiring needs, recruitment, retention, and attrition.
- Why it matters: Shows that an NNSA workforce-planning record can describe a site-specific contractor workforce and skills mix; it is not a public reconciliation of the federal 1,755-employee count and the 2,003-FTE federal request.
- Claim IDs: C8, C9
