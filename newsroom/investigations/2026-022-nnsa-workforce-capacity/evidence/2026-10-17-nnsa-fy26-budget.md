# E2 — NNSA FY2026 Congressional Justification

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/doe-fy-2026-vol-1-nnsa.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-17
- Locator: “NNSA Federal Salaries and Expenses” section, page 15 of the Budget in Brief.
- Excerpt: The FY2026 FSE request is $555,000,000 and supports a federal staff of 2,003 FTEs. The request includes Savannah River Operations Office FTEs previously funded by Defense Environmental Cleanup after responsibility for the Savannah River Site transferred to NNSA. NNSA says it will reshape its workforce through attrition, reductions, and streamlined mission support while allowing limited targeted growth.
