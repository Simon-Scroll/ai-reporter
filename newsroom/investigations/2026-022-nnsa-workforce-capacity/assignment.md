## Score note

GAO's 11.9 percent reduction is the source report's finding. The potentially new comparison is between GAO's Q2 FY2026 actual count of 1,755 federal employees and DOE/NNSA's FY2026 request for 2,003 federal FTEs. The comparison is only valid if the SRS transfer and workforce definitions are accounted for; it does not establish mission failure.

## Hypothesis

NNSA's FY2026 budget requested funding for 2,003 federal FTEs while GAO reports 1,755 federal employees at the end of the second quarter of FY2026; the difference may show that the agency's staffing plan and its reported workforce had diverged during a period of expanding nuclear-modernization work.

## What would be new

The records may establish a reproducible 248-position difference between a current NNSA staffing request and the agency's reported Q2 actual workforce, while also showing whether the two numbers include the same Savannah River workforce.

## Human stake

The federal staff directs and oversees a nuclear-security enterprise with a reported $25 billion annual budget and roughly 70,000 contractor personnel.

## Why it might be wrong

The budget request includes 38 Savannah River Operations Office FTEs transferred from DOE's environmental-cleanup account, and the GAO actual may use a different workforce scope or date. A budget target is not a staffing requirement, and the records do not establish that any mission or safety outcome followed from the gap.
