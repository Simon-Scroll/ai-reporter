# Evidence log

## E1 — GAO-27-109127, NNSA workforce reductions

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-109127
- Retrieved: 2026-10-17
- Locator: “What GAO Found” and “Why GAO Did This Study.”
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports that NNSA's federal workforce peaked at 1,991 early in FY2025 and then fell by 236 employees, or 11.9 percent, to 1,755 through the second quarter of FY2026. GAO attributes the reduction primarily to very limited hiring and employee separations, primarily retirements and resignations.
- Why it matters: Supplies the current actual workforce count and period for comparison. The report's 11.9 percent reduction is not being republished as the investigation's finding.

## E2 — DOE/NNSA FY2026 Congressional Justification

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/doe-fy-2026-vol-1-nnsa.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-17
- Locator: “NNSA Federal Salaries and Expenses” section, FY2026 Congressional Justification, page 15 of the Budget in Brief.
- Captured excerpt: “The FY 2026 Request for NNSA Federal Salaries and Expenses (FSE) is $555,000,000” and “supports a federal staff of 2,003 Federal FTEs.” The same section says the request includes Savannah River Operations Office FTEs previously funded by Defense Environmental Cleanup after responsibility for the Savannah River Site transferred to NNSA. It says NNSA will reshape its workforce through attrition, reductions, and streamlined mission support while allowing limited targeted growth.
- Why it matters: Supplies the independent budgeted staffing level and the scope caveat needed to test the apparent 248-position difference.

## E3 — DOE FY2026 FSE justification

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/doe-fy-2026-vol-1-fse.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-10-17
- Locator: “Highlights of the FY 2026 Request” under NNSA Federal Salaries and Expenses.
- Captured excerpt: The FY2026 FSE request is $555 million for 2,003 federal FTEs, including 38 additional FTEs at the Savannah River Operations Office previously funded through Defense Environmental Cleanup. NNSA says it will use attrition, reductions, and streamlined mission support for limited targeted growth.
- Why it matters: Confirms the workforce count and transfer detail in the dedicated FSE justification, rather than relying only on the summary budget volume.
