# Timeline

- 2026-10-17 — The October 17 horizon surfaced GAO-27-109127, published October 15 and publicly released October 16.

- 2026-10-17 — The investigation opened to test whether the reported Q2 actual workforce and the budget request share a denominator, including the 38 Savannah River Operations Office FTEs transferred from DOE Environmental Management.
- 2026-10-18 — NNSA's 2024 Year in Review supplied a distinct planning record: the Strategic Workforce Plan identifies federal workforce needs through FY 2031 and serves as the basis of NNSA's staffing plan.
- 2026-10-18 — NNSA's Savannah River Site contract workforce-planning requirements and the FY2025 Stockpile Stewardship and Management Plan were reviewed to distinguish contractor staffing plans and the larger management-and-operating workforce from the federal FTE denominator.
- 2026-10-18 — The same-object test passed for a labeled reconciliation gap: the records join on NNSA's federal workforce, but the public materials reviewed do not reconcile the forward plan with the Q2 actual or disclose whether the 38 transferred Savannah River positions are in the GAO count.
