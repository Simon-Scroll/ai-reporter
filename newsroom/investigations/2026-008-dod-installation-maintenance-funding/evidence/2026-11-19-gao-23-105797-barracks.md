# Evidence E11 — GAO-23-105797 military barracks condition records

- Retrieved: 2026-11-19

- Locator: printed pp. 11–14; scope and methodology, printed p. 3.
- Excerpt: GAO observed barracks at seven of 10 visited installations that appeared to require significant improvement despite condition scores above 80. At one installation, a barracks closed for renovation was described by installation officials as uninhabitable even though its service documentation showed a condition score above 90; repairs were estimated at about $5 million. GAO also reports that Army barracks assessments can be required at intervals from five to 10 years, depending on the building system.
- Boundary: The 10-installation sample was non-generalizable. This is evidence about the reliability and timeliness of barracks condition scores, not a department-wide estimate of FSRM execution or backlog reduction.
