# Timeline

- 2024 — FAA published the FY 2025–2029 National Airspace System Capital Investment Plan.
- 2025-07 — Congress appropriated $12.5 billion for BNATCS, according to GAO.
- 2025-12-11 — USAspending records the start of the Peraton BNATCS integrator indefinite-delivery vehicle.
- 2026-03 — GAO reports FAA had obligated $1.8 billion, or 20 percent of the appropriations assigned to the phase-1 modernization programs it reviewed.
- 2026-06 — GAO records FAA's high-level phase-1 systems-modernization estimate of $10.6 billion and says the agency could not provide supporting analysis.
- 2026-09-15 — The desk opened this investigation after comparing GAO's detailed cost and schedule findings with FAA's fact sheet, older capital plan, and current USAspending award records.
