# Evidence log

## E1 — GAO-26-107992

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107992
- PDF: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-107992.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-15
- Locator: report pages i–ii, 15, 20, 23–24, 26–34.
- Excerpt: GAO reports phase 1 completion targeted for December 2028, a $10.6 billion June 2026 high-level estimate for phase-1 systems modernization, approximately $10.2 billion for phase 2 systems modernization excluding facilities, and approximately $876 million as the final negotiated price for the integrator. GAO says the phase-1 estimate omits government costs, most operations and maintenance costs, and all phase-2 costs; it also says FAA had 11,389 nonintegrated project schedules as of May 2026.
- Boundary: GAO's report is proof of what it reviewed and found, not proof that the current contract totals are comparable to its program estimates.

## E2 — FAA BNATCS fact sheet

- URL: https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/fact_sheets/BNATCS_fact_sheet.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-15
- Locator: PDF pages 1–2, sections “What are we doing?”, “How is the contract structured?”, and “Where is the funding from?”
- Excerpt: FAA states that BNATCS will be delivered by the end of 2028, that a single integrator will manage the effort, and that Congress provided a $12.5 billion investment while an additional $20 billion will be needed to complete the entire program.
- Boundary: The fact sheet is an FAA summary and does not define the accounting scope of the $20 billion or disclose the detailed contract transaction basis.

## E3 — FAA FY 2025–2029 CIP

- URL: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/cip/media/CIP-Overview-2024.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-15
- Locator: PDF pages 1–2, 4, 10–13, and 16–18.
- Excerpt: FAA describes the CIP as a five-year plan for NAS capital investments and says proposed investments require lifecycle cost and schedule estimates and disclosure of interdependencies before Joint Resources Council review. The plan's roadmaps list legacy programs such as ERAM, STARS, TFMS, E-IDS, and TFDM across a 2021–2035 planning horizon.
- Boundary: This plan predates BNATCS and does not establish that its legacy programs map one-for-one to BNATCS phase 1.
