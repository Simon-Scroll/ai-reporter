# Hypothesis

FAA's accelerated BNATCS schedule and funding narrative may not yet be reconciled with the agency's contract-level commitments and cost baselines.

The initial primary comparison is deliberately limited. GAO reports a $10.6 billion high-level estimate for phase 1 systems modernization, approximately $10.2 billion for phase 2 systems modernization excluding facilities, and a negotiated integrator price of approximately $876 million. FAA's fact sheet separately describes a $12.5 billion congressional investment and says an additional $20 billion will be needed for the whole program.

The investigation will test whether the contract records, delivery-order values, phase estimates, and funding descriptions refer to the same scope and accounting basis. It will not infer waste, fraud, delay, or safety failure from a difference between an IDV ceiling, a delivery-order obligation, and a program estimate.
