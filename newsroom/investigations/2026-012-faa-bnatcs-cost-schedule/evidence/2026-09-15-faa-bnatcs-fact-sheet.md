# Evidence E2 — FAA BNATCS fact sheet

- URL: https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/fact_sheets/BNATCS_fact_sheet.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-15
- Locator: PDF pages 1–2, sections “What are we doing?”, “How is the contract structured?”, and “Where is the funding from?”
