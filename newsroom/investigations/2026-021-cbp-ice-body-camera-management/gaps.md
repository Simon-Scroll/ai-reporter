# Gaps

- GAO identifies stale CBP and ICE program plans and missing office-level guidance, but the plans themselves and later agency responses were not located in today's official-document search.
- ICE Directive 19010.3 assigns the OFTP Program Management Office responsibility for managing and overseeing the program, but it does not itself establish that an updated program management plan exists.
- CBP Directive 4320-030B requires office SOPs before deployment and assigns update, monitoring, and audit duties, but the directive is overarching guidance rather than a deployment schedule or results plan.

## Decision required

Locate the underlying plans or official recommendation-response records before deciding whether the contrast is a publishable same-object gap. Do not treat the existence of current directives as proof that plans or office guidance were updated, and do not treat their absence from indexed public pages as proof that agencies failed to create them.

## Decision — 2026-10-16

**Park.** The desk made a distinct same-day official retrieval pass. The GAO product HTML returned 403, so both official PDF paths were tried; `www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108157.pdf` also returned 403, while `files.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108157.pdf` was readable. The report's appendix confirms DHS agreed with six recommendations and described planned actions, but it does not provide the updated CBP or ICE program plans sought here. An official ICE statement dated August 8 said the agency was on track to equip every field officer and agent by the end of August, but supplied no completion count or revised plan. CBP's current public release page documents footage activity but still describes the program as a pilot and exposes no dated deployment baseline or office-specific guidance.

The same-object test therefore still fails: the public records show a later ICE assertion and continuing CBP operations, but do not supply a comparable current plan, completion record, cost/schedule baseline, or recommendation-response implementation record. A missing public document is not being treated as proof of noncompliance. Reopen only when CBP or ICE publishes an updated plan, a dated implementation/recommendation response, or a verifiable deployment-status record that can be compared with GAO-26-108157.
