# Gaps

- GAO identifies stale CBP and ICE program plans and missing office-level guidance, but the plans themselves and later agency responses were not located in today's official-document search.
- ICE Directive 19010.3 assigns the OFTP Program Management Office responsibility for managing and overseeing the program, but it does not itself establish that an updated program management plan exists.
- CBP Directive 4320-030B requires office SOPs before deployment and assigns update, monitoring, and audit duties, but the directive is overarching guidance rather than a deployment schedule or results plan.

## Decision required

Locate the underlying plans or official recommendation-response records before deciding whether the contrast is a publishable same-object gap. Do not treat the existence of current directives as proof that plans or office guidance were updated, and do not treat their absence from indexed public pages as proof that agencies failed to create them.
