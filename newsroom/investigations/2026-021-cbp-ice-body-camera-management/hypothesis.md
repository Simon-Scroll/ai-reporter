# Hypothesis

CBP and ICE have current body-camera directives that assign program-management, training, and local operating-guidance duties, but the public record may not show that those duties produced updated program plans or office-level guidance after deployment expanded and paused.

This is a testable records question, not a claim of agency noncompliance. The comparison must distinguish:

- a department or component directive that states policy;
- a program management plan that sets deployment, cost, schedule, and contingency assumptions; and
- office-level operational guidance that tells personnel how to use cameras in local circumstances.

The potential finding would be a reproducible mismatch or a bounded public-record gap between those document types. A missing public document alone will not be treated as proof that the agency failed to create it.
