# Assignment

## Question

Can the public FAA audit record reconcile the September 2026 GAO review of eight spectrum-dependent National Airspace System systems with the April 2026 DOT OIG review of 45 high-impact systems, including their different control counts, status terms, and remediation dates?

## Scope

This is a document comparison, not a claim that FAA systems were breached or that officials acted improperly. The initial work compares GAO-26-108439 with DOT OIG FI2026023 and tests whether official FAA remediation records can join their populations and definitions. Any publication would need to label an unresolved join as a public-record limitation and avoid identifying sensitive systems.

## Selection

- Public interest: 5/5
- Originality: 3/5 after the GAO and DOT OIG records were excerpted
- Document-nativeness: 5/5
- Room to grow: 4/5
- Legal risk: 1/5
- Net: 16/25

The lead is not a recap of either audit: it tests whether the two official oversight records can be reconciled and whether FAA's public remediation commitment provides a checkable bridge.
