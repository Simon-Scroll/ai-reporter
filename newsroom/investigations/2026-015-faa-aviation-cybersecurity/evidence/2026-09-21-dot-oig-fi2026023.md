## Locators and excerpts

- **Highlights, pp. 5–8:** DOT OIG reviewed 45 high-impact systems and reported 15 still using NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 4, plus 1,836 of 16,245 required controls not fully implemented. The report says 717 were documented as “not implemented” and 1,119 as “planned,” and that 38 of 45 systems lacked updated documentation for required high-baseline controls.
- **pp. 9–12, “FAA Does Not Fully Track”:** FAA primarily used its internal SMART system instead of DOT's CSAM system of record. The report says all systems supporting Automation, Communication, and Navigation and Weather had vulnerabilities not reported, tracked, and mitigated in CSAM; 14 of 17 Surveillance systems also had such vulnerabilities. The public exhibit with detailed counts is redacted.
- **pp. 13–14, recommendations and agency response:** DOT OIG made four recommendations covering Rev. 5 control selection and implementation, updated system security plans, transfer of vulnerabilities from SMART to CSAM, and accurate control-status documentation. FAA concurred and proposed full implementation by December 31, 2026.
- **Exhibits C–F, pp. 18–25:** the report identifies a 45-system population and control-status exhibits, but the public version redacts sensitive system details.
