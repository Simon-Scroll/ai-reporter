# Hypothesis

FAA's public audit record may contain a persistent, unjoinable boundary between GAO's September 2026 review of eight spectrum-dependent National Airspace System systems and DOT OIG's April 2026 review of 45 high-impact systems. The two official records report different control populations, implementation-status language, and review dates, while both identify outdated or incomplete security documentation.

This is a testable hypothesis, not a claim that FAA systems were breached or that the audits conflict. It could be disproved if a public FAA or DOT remediation record identifies the relationship between the samples and supplies a common control-status basis.
